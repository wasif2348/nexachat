Welcome to NexaChat v2.0
========================

Hello Team,

We are thrilled to announce the official launch of NexaChat — our new enterprise
messaging platform built for speed, collaboration, and security.

Key Features:
  - Real-time messaging with typing indicators
  - Organised channel rooms (General, Announcements, Tech-Talk, Random)
  - User profiles with custom avatars
  - File sharing and search
  - Role-based access control (Admin / User)

Getting Started:
  1. Log in with your credentials
  2. Join #general to introduce yourself
  3. Check #announcements for the latest updates

If you experience any issues, contact the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@nexacorp.internal
